# 60-90 Second Demo Script

1. **Problem, 0-10s:** "People need a clear way to understand cyclone movement and what to prepare without confusing a prototype with an official warning."
2. **Status, 10-25s:** Open Overview. Point out the DEMO MODE badge, current wind, pressure, movement, and the data provenance card.
3. **Track, 25-40s:** Open Cyclone Tracker. Explain the solid observed demo track, dashed prototype estimate, and shaded uncertainty concept.
4. **Risk, 40-55s:** Choose Kakinada Coast in Regional Risk. Explain that the score is exposure prioritization, not predicted damage, then point to the factor contributions.
5. **Preparedness, 55-70s:** Open Preparedness Planner and show Residents, Accessibility, Fishermen, Farmers, and Response teams guidance.
6. **Trust, 70-85s:** Open Model Insights and About & Sources. Explain that the fallback is rule-based, official/live feeds are not configured, and no unsupported accuracy claim is made.

Suggested closing: "CycloneShield AI detects context, explains uncertainty, and supports preparedness locally, while keeping official warnings and human decisions in charge."
